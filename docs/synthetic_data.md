# Synthetic Data

> **Everything in the `raw_synthetic` schema is SYNTHETIC.** It is generated by
> `afripulse.synthetic`, calibrated to real public statistics, and describes no
> real person, merchant or transaction. It must never be presented as real-world
> observation. Every row carries `is_synthetic = true` (enforced by a CHECK
> constraint) and the `generator_run_id` of the audit run that produced it.

## Why synthetic data?

Transaction-level retail, mobile money and loyalty data is confidential and not
publicly available. Synthetic data lets AfriPulse demonstrate transactional
modelling (facts, dimensions, incremental loads, loyalty analytics) while staying
honest: the *patterns* are anchored in real statistics, the *records* are invented.

## How it is generated

| Step | What happens |
|---|---|
| Settings | `config/synthetic.yml`: seed, period, countries, volumes, loyalty rules |
| Calibration | Latest real values read from the warehouse for each country (below) |
| Generation | `afripulse.synthetic.generators`: one seeded NumPy random generator |
| Invariants | `afripulse.synthetic.invariants`: consistency rules; any violation blocks the load |
| Load | PostgreSQL `COPY`, replacing the previous dataset in one transaction |
| Skip | If settings and calibration are unchanged (SHA-256 fingerprint), the run is skipped |

**Reproducible:** the same seed and calibration always produce identical data.

## Calibration (real statistics → synthetic behaviour)

| Real statistic | Source | Drives |
|---|---|---|
| Mid-year population | UN WPP 2024 (`TPopulation1July` × 1000) | Customers per country (with a minimum per country) |
| GDP per capita (US$) | World Bank `NY.GDP.PCAP.CD` | Typical purchase size: `1.5 × √(GDP per capita / 100)`, bounded to $2–$40 |
| Mobile money account share | Findex 2025 `mobileaccount.t.d` | Share of customers with mobile money, and how often they pay with it |
| Financial institution account share | Findex 2025 `fiaccount.t.d` | Share with a bank account, and card / bank transfer use |

The values actually used, and where each came from (or `default` if missing),
are stored in `raw_synthetic.calibration`.

## Realistic patterns

- **Amounts:** log-normal (many small purchases, few large), scaled by merchant category
  (e.g. electronics × 6, airtime × 0.4).
- **Timing:** busier Fridays and Saturdays, month-end salary peak (days 25–31 × 1.3),
  December peak (× 1.4), lunchtime and after-work hours.
- **Payments:** chosen from what *both* the customer and the merchant can use.
- **Mobile money:** P2P transfers, cash-in, cash-out, airtime, bill payments, plus a
  `merchant_payment` mirroring every retail purchase paid by mobile money; ~2% failures.
- **Loyalty:** members earn 10 points per US$1 and may redeem 500 points once eligible;
  unredeemed points accumulate (a realistic "breakage" liability).

## Tables and grain

| Table | Grain (one row per…) | Key |
|---|---|---|
| `calibration` | country × metric used in calibration | (`country_iso3`, `metric`) |
| `customers` | synthetic customer | `customer_id` |
| `merchants` | synthetic merchant | `merchant_id` |
| `retail_transactions` | purchase | `txn_id` |
| `mobile_money_transactions` | wallet transaction | `mm_txn_id` |
| `loyalty_events` | points earned or redeemed | `event_id` |

## Invariants (always true, checked before every load)

- Primary keys are unique; every foreign key points to an existing row.
- Customers only buy from merchants in their own country.
- No mobile money payment without a mobile money account; no card payment without a bank account.
- Mobile money transactions belong only to mobile money users.
- Amounts are positive; purchases fall inside the period and after the customer signed up.
- Loyalty balances never go negative; only members earn or redeem points.

## Default volume (config/synthetic.yml)

5,000 customers in 8 countries over 2024–2025: about 210,000 purchases,
280,000 mobile money transactions and 160,000 loyalty events (~650,000 rows,
generated and loaded in under a minute on a laptop).
